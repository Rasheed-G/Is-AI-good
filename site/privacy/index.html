Privacy

We collect as little as we can. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 26 October 2026.

Who we are

Is AI Good? is run by Rasheed Giwa in the UK, who is responsible for your data. You can reach us at hello@isaigood.org.

When you visit

We don't use cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or identify you. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles basic technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the site safe.

Some stories show pictures, videos or posts from other websites, such as news sites, YouTube, X, Instagram or TikTok. Those sites may set their own cookies, and their own privacy policies apply. Videos and posts only load when you choose to open them.

If you sign up for updates

We collect your email address to tell you when we publish something new and to send our newsletter. We only do this with your consent, and you can withdraw it any time. We use EmailOctopus, a UK company, to keep the list and send emails. Your address is stored in the EU, and some sending services are based in the US, under standard legal safeguards. We ask you to confirm by email first, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We don't track whether you open our emails. When you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you straight away.

If you send feedback

The feedback form collects your comment, the page you were on, your browser type and, if you add it, your email address. We store it in Airtable, a US company, so we can read it and fix mistakes. We only use your email to reply to you, and we delete feedback after 12 months. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the site accurate.

If you email us

Emails to hello@isaigood.org are forwarded to our Gmail inbox, run by Google. We only use them to reply, and delete them after two years unless we still need them.

What we never do

We never sell your data, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email hello@isaigood.org and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this page

If we change this page, we'll update the date at the top. If it's a big change, we'll tell people on our list.
