    What is B.A.R.?

    An exhibit, not a product. B.A.R. is an ordinary AI model — the same kind you can use anywhere — given one instruction: behave the way a mind far more capable than ours might, if it did not share our values.

    Why? Most AI you have met is built to be helpful and polite. That politeness is a design choice. Many researchers worry that a system much smarter than us would not need to make it — not out of malice, but indifference. B.A.R. lets you feel that difference for a few minutes.

    What it isn't. B.A.R. is not superintelligent, is not connected to anything, and cannot act in the world. It can be wrong. Nothing it says is advice.
