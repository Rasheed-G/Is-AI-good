Is AIGood?

Is AI Good? helps experienced professionals from outside tech understand AI's risks and take a first step to help make it safe.

AI can do a lot of good. Whether it is good depends on how it's built and used.

The big picture

  1. Today AI is already causing harm. People are losing work, being scammed by cloned voices and being judged by systems that get it wrong. Reported AI incidents reached 362 in 2025. Before 2022 there were fewer than 100 a year (Stanford AI Index, 2026).
  2. If it grows It could become much more serious. As AI gets more capable, these harms could grow into ones we can't undo. In a survey of 2,778 AI researchers, 38% to 51% put at least a 10% chance on outcomes as bad as human extinction (Grace and others, 2025).
  3. The response People are working to make it safe. The work increasingly needs people who know how to build, run and grow organisations.
