Is AIGood?

We're here to help professionals from outside tech understand AI, the risks that come with building it, and how it can be made safe.

The big picture

  1. Today AI is already causing harm. People are losing work, being scammed by cloned voices and being judged by systems that get it wrong.
  2. If it grows It could become much more serious. As AI gets more capable, some leading researchers warn these harms could grow into ones we can't undo.
  3. The response People are working to make it safe. The work increasingly needs people who know how to build, run and grow organisations.

What we do

  1. Show the harm AI is causing today, through real stories and clear explanations of what it means for people and their work.
  2. Explain where it could lead, and why some of the people building AI are worried.
  3. Help you learn how AI works and the risks that come with it, so you can make up your own mind.
  4. Help you find where someone with your experience fits, and take a first step to help make AI safe.

We're building this one step at a time. Today you can start with the harms.
