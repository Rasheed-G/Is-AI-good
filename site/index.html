Is AIGood?

We're here to help professionals from outside tech understand AI, the risks that come with building it, and how it can be made safe.

The big picture

AI is already causing harm. People are losing work, being scammed by cloned voices and being judged by systems that get it wrong. In the UK, 22% of employers say they've already cut jobs or hiring because of AI (King's College London, 2026).

It could become much more serious. As AI gets more capable, some leading researchers warn that harms like these could grow into ones we can't undo.

People are working to make it safe, and they need more help. Governments, researchers and new organisations are acting. The work increasingly needs people who know how to build, run and grow organisations.
