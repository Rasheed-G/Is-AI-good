About Is AI Good?

What we do

Is AI Good? helps experienced professionals from outside tech understand AI's risks and take a first step to help make it safe.

Why we exist

AI is getting more powerful faster than the work to make it safe, and some leading researchers warn the results could be catastrophic. A fast-growing field is working to prevent that. It has mostly been seen as a technical challenge, but it increasingly needs people who know how to build, run and grow organisations.

Who's behind it

I'm Rasheed Giwa. I founded Is AI Good? after 14 years helping research firms, an AI start-up and charities run and scale.

Connect with me on LinkedIn

Why I started it

When I began learning about AI safety, I kept hitting technical language with few plain-English explanations. I also saw that experienced professionals like me could help speed up the work, but had little to help them get started.

How we're funded

We're self-funded. If that changes, we'll say so here.

We use AI to help build this site, research and write. We link to our sources so you can check our claims.
